A coach for your own chess.com and Lichess games

Kibitz looks over your games afterwards, like a friend at the club: where each game turned, what you missed, and what to train next.

Use more than one device?

  • Every game, reviewed

    Stockfish checks each move on your device, and Kibitz tells you in plain words where the game turned. No AI, just the engine.

  • Training from your mistakes

    Puzzles from your own blunders, plus tactics, opening and endgame lessons pitched at your level.

  • Free and private

    Runs in your browser. No ads, no tracking, no account needed; sign in only if you want to sync devices.
