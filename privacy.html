Privacy

Last updated 17 November 2026 (opening courses added)

The short version

  • Without an account, nothing about you leaves your browser except the requests that fetch your public games from chess.com or Lichess. Your games, analyses and progress are stored only in this browser.
  • With an account (optional), your engine analyses, puzzle progress, baselines and the chess usernames you review are stored so they can sync across your devices. Your games are not uploaded.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no cookies for tracking. Nothing is sold or shared.
  • Comparing stats with friends is opt-in. Only friends you add see your numbers.
  • You can download or delete your account data yourself at any time.

Who is responsible

Kibitz is a free, non-commercial project run by its maintainer (GitHub: leo17coder), based in Denmark, who is the data controller for the optional accounts. Contact: open an issue at github.com/leo17coder/rapid-coach/issues.

Friends and the leaderboard (optional)

If you sign in, you get a private invite link. Anyone who opens it and signs in becomes your friend, and either of you can remove the other at any time. Making a new link stops the old one working.

If you switch on Share my stats with friends, these are stored and shown to your friends only:

  • the name you choose to show
  • which site you play on (chess.com or Lichess), but not your username there
  • per time control: games analysed, blunders and mistakes per game, median time used per game, puzzles solved, opening positions learned, lessons completed, your XP this week, your current streak, and your rating now and at your baseline

Your friends see your chosen name in their friends list even if you don't share. They never see your email or your games. Switching sharing off deletes your shared stats straight away. When you last switched sharing on or off is recorded, as a record of your choice.

Not stored: your games, your opponents' names, your IP address (beyond the short-lived logs any web host keeps), or anything about how you use the site.

Legal basis

Accounts are processed to provide the sync service you asked for (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Sharing stats with friends is based on your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), which you give and withdraw with the sharing switch. You can use Kibitz fully without an account.

Who processes the data

  • Supabase stores the account data in the EU and runs sign-in. It acts as a processor under a data processing agreement.
  • An email provider sends the sign-in emails, as a processor.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website itself.
  • chess.com and Lichess: your browser fetches your public games directly from them. Their own privacy policies apply.

How long it's kept

Until you delete your account. Accounts that haven't been used for a long time may be deleted; this page will say how long before that happens.

Your rights

  • Access and portability: Account → Download my data gives you everything stored, as a JSON file.
  • Deletion: Account → Delete account removes your account and all synced data immediately. Data in your browser stays until you clear it.
  • Correction, objection, restriction: contact the maintainer as above.
  • Complaints: you can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency, Datatilsynet.

Connecting Lichess (optional, opening trainer)

The opening trainer can fill gaps in your own games with the most common moves from Lichess games at your rating, using the Lichess opening explorer. Lichess only allows this for signed-in Lichess accounts, so you can choose Connect Lichess. You then sign in on lichess.org and Lichess gives this browser an access token without any extra permissions.

  • The token is stored only in this browser. It is never sent to Kibitz's account service and doesn't sync.
  • Each explorer request sends Lichess a chess position, the game speed and your rating band, with your token, so Lichess knows which account asked. Lichess's privacy policy applies.
  • Results are cached in this browser. Disconnect Lichess deletes the token and the cache here and asks Lichess to revoke the token.

Lessons and your weakness profile

Lesson suggestions are worked out in your browser: Stockfish checks the mistakes in your analysed games and fixed rules sort them by theme (for example hanging pieces or missed forks) and game phase. These tags stay in this browser with your games and are never uploaded. Only your lesson progress, puzzle levels and chosen level sync, if you have an account.

The lesson puzzles come from the Lichess puzzle database (public domain), and the opening names in opening lessons from the Lichess chess-openings list (public domain). Both are served from this site, so using them sends nothing to Lichess. Which lesson puzzles you solved or missed stays in this browser. "Drill this line in the opening trainer" saves the line's moves as your repertoire moves, which are part of your opening trainer progress (see the table above). Each puzzle shows a link to it on lichess.org; only clicking it opens Lichess.

Daily goal, streaks and badges

The Home tab's daily goal, streak, XP, levels, badges, weekly challenge and records are worked out in your browser from your own training. They're never given for wins, there are no notifications or emails, and you can hide them all on the Home tab. Without an account they stay in this browser; with an account they sync as described above. The date of your last visit, used for "Since your last visit", stays in this browser only.

No third-party requests

The site's fonts, icons, chess pieces and engine are served from the site itself, so opening Kibitz doesn't send your IP address to font or script hosts. The only outside requests are the ones described above (chess.com or Lichess for your games, and the account service and Lichess explorer if you use them).

Storage in your browser

Kibitz saves your games, analyses, settings (including display settings such as theme, board, pieces and sounds) and (if you sign in) your sign-in session in your browser's local storage and IndexedDB. This is needed for the features you use, so there's no cookie banner. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Age

Accounts are for people aged 13 and over.

Kibitz is not affiliated with chess.com or Lichess.
